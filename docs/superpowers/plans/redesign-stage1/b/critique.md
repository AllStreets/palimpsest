# Direction B, Hologram: critique by round

Judged against the spec's goals and anti-goals, CORANTO's bar and the five Review Focus lines, on the six stills from `tools/redesign-brief.ts stills` (port 4789, a scratch home, the GPU lock held).

What B is: the Brief as cool cyan and white light on smoked glass, chamfered at two corners, with lit edges and an emitter along its foot. Saira (its width axis: wide and light for the date, normal and medium for headlines) with Geist Mono for every figure. Keys lit from inside, in four weights. Copy cut hard in `brief-words.ts`: each row is a meta line, a headline and readouts; the sentence waits under Why.

## Round 1

What works:
- The date in wide, light Saira with a soft cyan bloom is the first thing that says "instrument", and it is unlike A's heavy mono.
- Rows are headline plus figures. The lead, the since line and the usage sentence are gone from view (kept for a screen reader); the head carries 06 waiting, 11 merged, 954.60 credits and two arc gauges with the cap as an amber tick.
- The spend key carries its sum in a recessed window (Post, 60 cr): the money is on the key that spends it.
- The grant key is white-hot in a halo with a lit seal; the agreement ring (48 of 50 ticks, the two misses amber) makes the case in one look. Review Focus 4 is met.
- The audit's claims read as one bar (real, partial, missing, broken).
- Over the overview (Review Focus 2) the text holds; at 1280 x 800 (Focus 5) nothing clips.

Weaknesses:
1. **The head is an opaque black slab.** The old "solid paper" rule behind the Brief's head still paints, so the top third is a flat black rectangle with a hard bottom edge. It kills the glass exactly where the eye lands. Anti-goal: flat panels.
2. **The depth is muddled, not layered.** The mid pane reads as a pale bezel round the panel, and a rounded glow corner shows at the top right against the chamfer. The far plane's grid sits behind the panel and shows through the slips as a lighter vertical band (x 735 to 930). The ghost numeral never shows. Nothing reads as a pane at another depth.
3. **Still a strip of boxes.** Proposal rows carry Why, Tier, Dismiss and Post, three of them boxed; at a glance Tier and Dismiss weigh almost as much as Post. Anti-goal: boring buttons, dashboard kit.
4. **The autonomy ask says its evidence three times**: the ring, "10/10 on this repo" under it, and "48/50 agreed, 10/10 here" again as readouts.
5. **Every section has the same slips.** Overnight's merges sit on the same lifted glass as what waits on him, so the page is one long stack of equal cards and the city never reads through the body. The spec asks for nearer and farther, and for the city clear through the glass.
6. **Low-value readouts.** "harness" and "README audit" on every proposal repeat what Why says; "market off" under "halted" is a second word for one fact.
7. **The since figure has no label**: "yesterday 06:40" floats under the figures.
8. **Rows are tall** (about 135 px for a proposal): a blank band between the headline and its readouts.

## Round 2

Changes from round 1: the head stands on the glass, not on a black slab; the mid pane is a second chamfered sheet set up and to the right, its own lit edge and cut showing; the day's numeral is projected into the sky beside the glass on a fading grid; deciding keys lose their rim (only spending and granting are keys you see from across the room); Overnight, Recommended and In the city stand back with no slip of their own, a hairline between rows, so the city reads through the body; readouts that repeated Why are cut (harness, README audit, market off); the ring is the autonomy ask's only figure; the since figure is labelled.

What works now:
- The whole panel is glass: the date and the readouts sit on light, and the towers read through the farther sections. This is the direction's promise met.
- Rows sort at a glance: lit Post keys with their sums, then quiet words.
- Review Focus 1: the scrolled end is clean to its last row, inside the glass.

Weaknesses:
1. **The numeral in the sky is decoration, and it fights the city.** Over the overview the "20" cuts straight through the hero tower. It says only the date the head already says. Anti-goal: neon kitsch, and it breaks "the city reads clearly".
2. **The depth is still mostly the mid pane's thin edge.** The head and the list are one surface. The spec asks for panels at several depths; only the autonomy pane is nearer.
3. **The audit row says its claims twice**: the bar and its key, then "14 claims" again in the readouts.
4. **The section note wraps** ("Nothing runs until you / post.") in mono at a short measure: a rag that reads as an accident.
5. **A clause readout** ("Merged with changes") is set like a figure, in ice mono, the same as "33.34 cr"; words and figures should not share a face and colour.
6. **Handled rows keep their tier readout**, which is no longer the news once the job is posted.

## Round 3

Changes from round 2: the numeral in the sky is gone (one accessory removed); the head is now its own pane, nearer than the list, proud of the glass at the left and casting a shadow down onto it, with its own lit top and left edges, and it follows the pointer more than the glass does; the audit's claims are said once, by the bar, with only "4 jobs proposed" as a readout; the section note is one line; a clause readout is set in the display face and frost, so words and figures no longer share a voice; handled rows drop their readouts.

What works now:
- Three depths read at once: the head pane nearest, the glass with its slips, the mid pane's lit edge behind, and the city through all of them. The spec's "layered panels at different depths" is visible in a still, not only on hover.
- Over the overview (Review Focus 2) the city is clear right of the glass and through the farther sections, and no text loses to the brightest towers: the slips and the head pane keep their own dark glass under the words.
- The scrolled end (Review Focus 1) is a calm list: hairlines, light edges, figures, quiet keys, and the city through it.

Weaknesses:
1. **The title fell back to the serif.** Cutting the old glow rule took the display face's title rule with it: "Friday 20 November" is Newsreader again. A quality fault at the anti-goal level (it reads as unfinished, two languages on one pane).
2. **The head pane's top right corner meets the glass's chamfer** and the two cuts cross into a muddle.
3. **The autonomy ask sits at the foot of the first screen**, its grant key under the body's fade. Review Focus 4 asks that it read as the heaviest action; on the first screen it is half seen.
4. **With reduced motion the mid pane keeps an old 14 px offset**, so it sits a little off from where the animated still leaves it.
5. **At 1280 x 800 the head pane is tall** (about a third of the window), leaving room for only three rows.

## Round 4

Changes from round 3: the title is Saira again, wide and light; the head pane stops short of the chamfer; the autonomy ask stands first under Waiting on you (the order of everything else is the engine's, and no action changes); the reduced-motion mid pane sits still where it belongs; at narrow widths the head pane tightens.

Against the anti-goals, nothing left at that level:
- Low quality: every object has an edge light, a depth and a shadow; no default control remains.
- Boring fonts: Saira across its width axis (125% light for the date, 100 to 112% medium for headlines, section names and keys) with Geist Mono for every figure. Plex and Newsreader are gone from the Brief.
- Boring buttons: four weights, each built differently: looking is a word on a lit baseline; deciding a frosted key lit faintly from under its face; spending a dark key lit from inside with its sum in a recessed window; granting the one white-hot key in a double halo with a lit seal.
- Boring colours: cyan and white light on smoked teal glass, with mint, amber and coral kept for state.
- No depth: the head pane, the glass and its slips, the mid pane, the city; the autonomy pane raised in front of its section; parallax on hover moves each at its own depth.
- Simple code: the agreement ring (one tick a decision, the misses amber, the run here as a dashed inner ring), the claims bar and the arc gauges with the cap tick carry the information the sentences used to.
- Retro: no grain, scanlines or CRT blur; one projection on open, from the emitter up.

Review Focus, all five: the scrolled end stays inside the glass to Today's limit (1); over the overview the text holds and the city reads (2); with reduced motion the page is whole at 0.7 s, with no projection and no parallax (3); the autonomy ask is first, raised and the only white key (4); at 1280 x 800 nothing clips (5).

What remains, below the anti-goal level, for Stage 2 if this direction is picked:
1. A proposal row still carries four controls (Why, Tier, Dismiss, Post). Only Post is a key you see from across the room, but three rows of them repeat.
2. The readouts parse the engine's sentences (credits paid, PR number, attempts left, audit counts, agreement). Stage 2 should give the Brief these as fields, so a change of wording cannot blank a figure.
3. Rows with no Why lose their sentence entirely; for a merge the figures say it all, but a milestone or a graduate row may need its words one tap away by another route.
4. The motion (the projection on open, the parallax, the waiting pulse) never shows in a still; the pick sheet should say so.
5. The mid pane is subtle: its lit edge shows at the top right, but over a bright sky it could read as a stray line.

## The perf gate

`tools/redesign-brief.ts frames`, uncapped (`REDESIGN_UNCAPPED=1`), on B's final build, then main (4c19608) re-run in the same sitting:

| Tier | View | Baseline (baseline.md) | Main, same sitting | B final |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| high | city alone | 8.60 ms (p95 9.80) | 8.70 ms (p95 9.70) | 8.70 ms (p95 9.70) |
| high | Brief open | 8.10 ms (p95 9.20) | 7.80 ms (p95 8.80) | 7.80 ms (p95 8.90) |
| medium | city alone | 4.90 ms (p95 6.10) | 4.80 ms (p95 6.00) | 5.00 ms (p95 5.90) |
| medium | Brief open | 4.60 ms (p95 5.80) | 4.60 ms (p95 6.30) | 4.60 ms (p95 5.90) |

The Brief open costs what main's does at both tiers, well inside the 10% gate. The city-alone rows sit within 0.2 ms of every baseline run, run-to-run noise: B touches nothing on the city's path, and its panes are display: none while the Brief is closed.

What keeps it cheap: no backdrop-filter anywhere (A's lesson); the glass is gradients over the live city, the glows are static box-shadows painted once, the waiting pulse animates opacity on a 6 px dot, and parallax writes two custom properties on a frame only while the pointer moves, with transforms alone following them.
